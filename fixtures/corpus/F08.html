Rival plans: what electric scooter riders will actually pay

Our price check of the new subscription services in the city found that VoltRide's entry level plan will cost Rs. 1,599 a month once the standard battery rental is added to the advertised fee, well above what some early posts suggested. Competitors price similar offers in a tight band, and analysts say the difference will matter most to delivery riders who count every rupee. The reporter visited three neighbourhood dealers and compared the written quotes with the app screens. Whether the higher figure reflects a launch surcharge or a permanent price is unclear, and the company did not respond to questions about billing before publication. We will update this comparison when the company publishes its full rate card for the city.
