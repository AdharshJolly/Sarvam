VoltRide launches scooter subscription in Bengaluru (F03)

Bengaluru commuters get another way to ride electric without buying a vehicle. VoltRide Mobility today announced the launch of its electric scooter subscription service in Bengaluru, starting with three hundred scooters across Koramangala, Indiranagar and Whitefield. Monthly plans start at Rs. 1,299 for a basic scooter with a standard battery, and the subscription bundles insurance, free servicing and a swappable battery at no extra charge. Riders can pause or cancel their plan with seven days notice, and a refundable deposit of Rs. 2,000 applies to every new account. The company plans to grow the fleet to two thousand scooters by the end of next year and to open six battery swap stations along the main commuter corridors of the city. Chief executive Arjun Rao said the launch reflects strong demand from delivery partners and office commuters who want predictable monthly costs. Copyright EV Wire. Republished with permission.
