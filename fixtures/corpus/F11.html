India micro-mobility market outlook

Market size depends on what is counted. Under the broad definition, which covers all shared micro-mobility including scooters, bikes and e-cycles, the Indian market is worth Rs. 5,000 crore a year. Under the narrow definition, which covers only e-scooter rentals and subscriptions, the market is worth Rs. 1,200 crore a year. Growth in both segments is expected to continue as cities add dedicated lanes and as fuel prices stay high. The report cautions that vendors often quote the broad figure when describing their own opportunity, and that buyers should check which definition a number uses before comparing two forecasts. The survey covered fourteen cities and was completed with the help of operators, regulators and riders between January and June.
