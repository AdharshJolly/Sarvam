Scooter geek: is VoltRide worth it?

I have been testing rental scooters around the city for a couple of years, so of course I tried the newest one. As per VoltRide Mobility's own release, the entry price is Rs. 1,299 every month and you can cancel whenever you like. My ride from Jayanagar to the office was smooth, the app was quick to unlock the scooter, and the battery lasted the whole day. I would still like to see how the service copes when a few thousand people sign up, and how quickly the support team answers when a scooter refuses to start in the rain. More notes after a month of daily use, including how the cost compares with owning a petrol scooter and paying for fuel, parking and yearly servicing.
