Zipwheel expands its rental network

Zipwheel, the city based scooter rental start up, said it now operates 500 scooters across eight zones and plans to double the fleet once its second charging depot opens. The founders said utilisation has been steady through the winter months, and that most riders are students and first jobbers who prefer to pay by the hour. The company reports that it has handled more than ten thousand rides since launch and that theft and vandalism have been lower than expected because every scooter carries a tracking unit. Investors in the round said they were encouraged by the early numbers and expected the business to reach break even within two years of operation if the current pace of rides continues without a big rise in maintenance costs.
