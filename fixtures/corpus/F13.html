A simple sponge cake

This sponge cake is easy to bake on a weekend. Mix two cups of flour with three eggs, one cup of sugar and a spoon of baking powder, then fold in melted butter until the batter is smooth. Pour it into a greased tin and bake for thirty minutes until a skewer comes out clean. Let the cake cool for ten minutes before turning it out, and dust it with icing sugar or top it with fresh cream and berries. Leftovers keep well for two days in an airtight container, and the cake also freezes nicely when sliced and wrapped tightly in foil, ready for an unexpected guest on a busy afternoon.
