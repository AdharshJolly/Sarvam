Daily pass option for occasional riders

Not everyone wants a monthly commitment. Occasional riders in the city can pick up the premium pass at Rs. 69 per day, which includes the longer range battery and theft protection, and pay only for the days they ride. The operator says the daily option is meant for visitors and for people testing the service before committing, and that regular commuters will find the monthly plans cheaper. A day pass is valid from the moment of unlock until midnight, and riders can extend it with a single tap in the app. Return the scooter to any of the marked parking zones to end the pass, and report damage within an hour so that the cost is not charged to your account.
