VoltRide pricing

Choose the plan that fits the way you ride. The basic plan costs Rs. 1,299 per month and includes a standard battery, roadside help and a monthly servicing visit. The premium plan costs Rs. 2,070 per month and adds a longer range battery, priority support and theft protection. Both plans need a refundable deposit, have no lock in period, and can be paused for up to thirty days a year. All prices include taxes. Prices shown are for new riders in Bengaluru and may change for other cities. Corporate and delivery partner plans are available on request from our sales team, and fleet discounts apply from ten scooters. Payments are collected automatically on the same date each month.
