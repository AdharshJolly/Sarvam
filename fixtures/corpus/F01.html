Notification on rental permits for electric two wheelers

Karnataka Transport Department. Notification on the regulation of electric two wheeler rental and subscription services within the limits of the Bengaluru metropolitan area. With effect from the first of April, every operator that rents or leases electric scooters to the public must hold a rental permit issued by the Transport Department, in addition to a valid trade licence from the local municipal authority. The rental permit is mandatory for each operator and is issued for a period of one year. The annual permit fee is Rs. 5,000 for each operator, payable through the department portal before the permit is activated. Every scooter in the fleet must be registered as a rental vehicle and must carry third party insurance for the entire period of the rental. Operators must report any accident involving a rental vehicle to the nearest regional transport office within forty eight hours of the incident and keep trip records for one year.
